Common questions

Why don’t I see any plans?

Pulse only matches you with friends and members of clubs you join. Add a few friends (Me → Friends) and set a signal on Week.

Can friends see when I say I’m not down?

No. Your answer to “Still down?” is private. A Pulse only forms when enough people say yes.

What’s the difference between Open and Quiet?

Friends can see Open signals. Quiet signals stay hidden unless a friend is compatible.

How does Free now work?

Tap Free now on Home, pick what you’re up for and for how long. Friends see you’re free until it runs out. Pulse never shares where you are.

How do I bring my club in?

Open your club (Me → Pulse Pods), tap Invite, and share your link. People who don’t have Pulse yet get the invite right after they sign up.

How do I report or block someone?

Tap their name in Friends, or the ••• menu in a Pulse room. You can also long-press a message to report it.

How do I delete my account?

Me → Settings & privacy → Delete account.
